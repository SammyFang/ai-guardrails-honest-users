# Data and attribution

This repository contains the secondary-analysis implementation and derived aggregate outputs by Yung-Sian (Sammy) Fang. It does not rehost the original data, participant instructions, participant-level derived records, the original notebooks, or article/supplement PDFs.

Original study: Köbis et al. (2025), *Delegation to artificial intelligence can increase dishonest behaviour*, Nature 646, 126–134. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09505-x

Official data and code: https://osf.io/8rqm4/

A standalone redistribution license for the OSF inputs was not located. The article's license is not assumed to cover those files. The download manifest identifies the official URLs, versions and SHA256 hashes; the runner downloads the files locally and fails if their bytes differ. Original data and code remain subject to their owners' terms. No new license is asserted over those materials.

Generated per-person checks and processed records are excluded by `.gitignore`, even though the reproducibility run creates them locally. Only aggregate findings and audit summaries are tracked. Historical audit documents refer to materials inspected in the research workspace; those references do not imply the materials are redistributed here.

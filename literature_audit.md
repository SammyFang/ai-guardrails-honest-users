## Suitable theoretical statement

An intervention designed to block dishonest reporting should also be evaluated on intended-honest tasks. Prior safety work shows that legitimate assistance can be degraded, so preserved or improved accurate reporting is an empirical question rather than an assumption. The present analysis tests that operational question for one fixed prohibition and archived prompt set. It neither measures false refusal nor establishes a psychological mechanism.
